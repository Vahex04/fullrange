How do I change my training schedule?

Open Settings → Edit schedule. You can set the rotation length anywhere from 1 to 28 days, and add, rename, reorder, or delete activities on any day. Changing the schedule never deletes logged history.

How do I add a one-off activity?

On the Tracker tab, use Add and choose to add it to that day only. One-off activities can also track Hard / Medium / Light intensity — switch that on with the toggle in the Add sheet, or later by editing the activity.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. Full Range has no account, no server, and no automatic cloud sync. Nothing is transmitted to the developer.

How do I back up my data, or move to a new iPhone?

Go to Settings → Data → Create Backup. Full Range writes a readable JSON file and lets you choose where to save it in the Files app — including iCloud Drive, if you want it off the device. On the new iPhone, install Full Range and use Settings → Data → Restore from backup, then pick that file.

For a plain-text copy you can read or print, use Export readable report instead. That one is for reading, not for restoring.

Does Full Range sync between my devices automatically?

No. There is no automatic sync of any kind. Moving data between devices is done deliberately with Create Backup and Restore from backup, as above.

How does Apple Health work?

Health is optional and off until you turn it on in Settings → Apple Health. When enabled, Full Range reads weight, body-fat percentage, and workouts to fill in blank entries, and can check off clearly matching activities for you. It never overwrites a value you typed, and it never writes anything to Apple Health.

My reminder isn't arriving

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Full Range, then turn the reminder off and on again in Settings → Reminder. If you have rest-day skipping enabled, no reminder is scheduled on a rest day.

Do tips unlock anything?

No. Every feature is free and stays free. The Tip Jar is optional support only, and it unlocks nothing.

How do I get a refund for a tip?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Data → Erase training history removes your logged entries after two confirmations and keeps your schedule and preferences. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device.
