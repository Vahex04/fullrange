FREQUENTLY ASKED

How do I change my training rotation?
Settings → Edit rotation. You can change the length (1 to 28 days), add
or remove activities on any day, rename them, and reorder them.

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, it is also
copied to your own private iCloud account. It is never sent anywhere
else.

How do I back up my data?
Turn on iCloud sync in Settings. You can also export everything as plain
text from Settings → Data → Export my data.

Do donations unlock anything?
No. Every feature is free and always will be. Donations are optional
support only. I am the sole developer on this app and I work hard to provide a quality free app to help people in the gym! 

How do I get a refund for a donation?
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer. Visit
reportaproblem.apple.com.
