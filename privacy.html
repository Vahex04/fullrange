Privacy Policy for Full Range

Last updated: 08/23/2026

Full Range does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information. There is no account, no login, and no server operated by
the developer. The developer never receives your data.

DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
All information you enter — your training rotation, completed
activities, intensity ratings, body weight, body fat percentage, notes,
and goals — is stored only on your iPhone.

ICLOUD
If you turn on iCloud sync, this same information is copied to your own
private iCloud account so it is backed up and available on your other
devices. This uses Apple's iCloud service under your own Apple Account.
The developer has no access to it. You can turn sync off at any time in
Settings within the app.

APPLE HEALTH
If you grant permission, Full Range reads body weight, body fat
percentage, and workout records from Apple Health in order to fill in
your log automatically. If you explicitly enable the option, it can also
save completed sessions back to Apple Health. Health data is used only
inside the app on your device. It is never used for advertising, never
shared with third parties, and never transmitted to the developer. You
can revoke access at any time in the iOS Health app under Sharing.

NOTIFICATIONS
If you enable the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled locally
on your device. No reminder data leaves your iPhone.

PURCHASES
Full Range offers optional donations through Apple's In-App Purchase
system. These unlock no features. Payments are processed entirely by
Apple; the developer receives no payment details, card information, or
personal information about you.

ANALYTICS AND ADVERTISING
Full Range contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no
third-party SDKs of any kind.

CHILDREN
Full Range does not knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children.

DELETING YOUR DATA
Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. You can also
erase everything from within the app at Settings, under Data. If iCloud
sync was enabled, removing the app's data from iCloud is done through
your Apple Account settings.
